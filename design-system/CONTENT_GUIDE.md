# MOVR Content Guide

Tone: energetic, youth-driven, pragmatic. Short sentences. Active voice. Zero fluff.

## Rules

### 1. Direct copy
- **Before:** Please confirm your pickup location when you are ready.
- **After:** Confirm pickup.

### 2. Drivers-first (subscriptions, not commission)
- **Before:** We take a small platform cut from each trip.
- **After:** Keep 100% of your trip earnings with a subscription.

### 3. Merchant narrative
- **Before:** Grow your business with our powerful SaaS tools.
- **After:** Sell faster with in-app storefronts and instant delivery.

### 4. Trust = concrete facts
- **Before:** We care about your safety.
- **After:** Fare shown before you book. ID verified. Live trip sharing.

## Surfaces

Apply this voice to app UI strings, push/inbox notifications, WhatsApp/Telegram/SMS booking messages, and admin copy — not only the marketing site.
